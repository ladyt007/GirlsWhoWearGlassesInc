Topics Covered in the Classes

The full expression of everything covered under "What You'll Learn" above — organized into six focused topics, from first impressions to formal dining, for students, young professionals, and adults ready to build lasting confidence.

Topic 1

Foundations & First Impressions

What etiquette really is, why heartfelt manners never go out of style, and how posture and presence create an instant, welcoming first impression.

Topic 2

The Handshake & Introductions

The mechanics of a confident handshake, plus how to introduce people gracefully and in the right order, in any setting.

Topic 3

Names & Conversation

Recovering smoothly when a name slips your mind, and reading the room to keep table conversation balanced and inclusive.

Topic 4

Digital Discretion & Style Foundations

Managing phones and devices respectfully, and building a polished, professional wardrobe foundation for women and men alike.

Topic 5

Style Pitfalls & Formal Dining

The wardrobe missteps that undercut a polished look, plus how to read a formal table and navigate proper napkin etiquette.

Topic 6

Dining Mechanics & Polished Finish

Bread, drinks, passing dishes, and soup — the small physical details of confident dining — and how to handle mistakes gracefully to close out any occasion.
